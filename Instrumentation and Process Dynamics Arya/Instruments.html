Thermistor

Change in temperature \(\alpha\) change in resistance offered. They are semiconductors with junctions (P-N) whose resistance varies as a function of temperature. There are 2 types - NTC : resistance decreases with time - PTC : resistance increases with time range: \(T<200\degree C\) uses: thermometers, heat detectors, etc. ## ICs ICs are integrated circuits. They are electronic components which detect the change in temperature. uses: Temperature sensors, pressure sensors, Humidity sensor, etc. Consists of semiconductor transistors, which generate voltage proportional to the intended property of measurement, eg: temperature. ## Pyrometers Devices used to measure temperature of very hot objects.
